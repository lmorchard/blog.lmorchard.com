## Adversarial Interoperability

Persona 5 Royal went on sale on Steam, and like a total sucker, I [bought it again](https://masto.hackers.town/@lmorchard/117080389493065295) for the third time. The problem was I already had 40+ hours sunk into the Switch version. I didn't want to start over. 

Luckily, my Switch is jailbroken. I backed up my saves and discovered that Switch P5R save files are directly compatible with the PC version. A little file copying later, and I had my 40 hours ported over to Steam. Adversarial interoperability wins again.

But it gets nerdier. The gaming PC lives down in the basement running Sunshine. There's a homebrew Moonlight client for the Switch. Naturally, I [started playing](https://masto.hackers.town/@lmorchard/117080401657039451) the PC version of the game on my Switch, streamed from the basement. I briefly considered running a Switch emulator on the PC and streaming *that* back to the physical Switch, but I'm pretty sure that violates the laws of thermodynamics.

Speaking of running things forever, my Tamagotchi has been [returning to the home planet](https://masto.hackers.town/@lmorchard/117085142622480374) for over two years now on the same battery.

If I had infinite money, I'd pay someone to find a pristine AMC Eagle and do a full EV conversion. (Or maybe a '77 Pinto Cruising Wagon, assuming the battery swap prevents it from exploding.)

## Working Memory Offload

I read [an article](https://davidepiffer.com/p/ai-isnt-outthinking-mathematicians) by Davide Piffer arguing that AI isn't necessarily out-thinking mathematicians; it's out-remembering them. AI models can hold hundreds of equations, problem statements, and abandoned paths in their context window all at once. 

It made me think about Julian Jaynes' *The Origin of Consciousness* — the idea that ancient humans operated with bicameral minds, where hallucinated gods gave orders instead of unified metacognition. It [started me wondering](https://masto.hackers.town/@lmorchard/117080669116086544) how much of our brain is rigid hardware and how much is cultural software. If AI removes the biological bottleneck of human working memory, what kind of minds will the next wave of humans host? 

In the meantime, the tools get weirder. Cloudflare [open-sourced an AI triage bot](https://thenewstack.io/cloudflare-astro-triage-bot/) that helped clear Astro's five-year issue backlog, and I stumbled on [intuition-probe](https://gist.github.com/JeremyTheocharis/83c76da5a10bcf495d4298c70fee91b4), a script that makes an LLM hallucinate the API it *expects* before seeing your actual code, so you can design for human intuition. It's a fascinating way to use hallucinations as a feature rather than a bug.

## Plums and Paws

We have a few fruit trees around the house. Most years, the dropping fruit is just a late-summer cleanup chore. This weekend I actually [collected some plums](https://masto.hackers.town/@lmorchard/117112631849074083). Chopped them up, threw them in a jar with sugar, and started making a shrub. 

When I do stuff like that, I [half-convince myself](https://masto.hackers.town/@lmorchard/117112643989922047) I should quit tech and just make physical things for a living. Then I remember tech pays for the house with the fruit trees.
